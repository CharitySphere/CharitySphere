About CharitySphere

CharitySphere is an AI-powered welfare ecosystem designed to overcome barriers in charitable giving by creating a centralized, transparent, and technology-driven platform.

We connect donors, volunteers, and institutions through a unified digital hub that ensures resources reach beneficiaries efficiently while maintaining transparency and trust.

Our platform integrates real-time donation tracking, surplus food collection, multilingual support, and emergency response systems to create meaningful social impact.
